About

Roots in hardware.
Canopy in agents.

Autobotanist started as a quiet GitHub Pages sketch and a monogram. The name stuck: cultivate carefully, automate what should compound, leave room for craft.

Today the same handle spans video tooling, GTM agent systems, comic research, RL verifiers, and older Arduino experiments—one continuous garden of systems work.

“Every dollar tied to muscle must eventually find its way to mind, or both will atrophy.”

— MrGoat / ObliviousOdin
