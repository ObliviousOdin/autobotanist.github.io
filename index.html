Mission

Food sovereignty for local communities

Autobotanist empowers local communities by providing an integrated platform that simplifies sustainable growing. We deliver infrastructure, IoT tools, and data-driven insights that connect urban farmers, hobbyists, and wholesalers — reducing barriers to a high-yield, organic lifestyle.

The problem

Local agriculture lacks the connectivity to scale

  • Consumers face high costs and environmental impact from non-local produce.
  • Local growers struggle with labor, technical complexity, and no dedicated marketplace.
  • Community agriculture stays fragmented — hard to grow, hard to sell, hard to share what works.
The solution

A multi-sided connected platform

Hardware, software, and open data — designed for people with non-backend coding knowledge.

Hardware

Modular IoT grow kits and sensors that automate water, light, and nutrient needs.

Software

Professional-grade tools and e-commerce so market vendors can sell effortlessly.

Data

Open growing data among users to optimize yields and eliminate trial-and-error.

Primary value prop

“5miles for Farmers”

An exclusive, simple-to-use platform for local vendors to reach their immediate community — not a global mega-marketplace. Professional-grade data analysis through an intuitive interface for growers who aren’t engineers.

Autobotanist strategic identity

Vision, mission, and positioning — the language we use with communities, partners, and investors.

Vision

To create a world where fresh, local food is the universal standard — a choice defined by taste and health rather than origin or environmental cost. We imagine a future where every household is part of a waste-free, connected ecosystem that empowers individuals to grow and consume medicine and food exactly where they live.

Mission

Autobotanist empowers local communities to achieve food sovereignty by providing an integrated platform that simplifies the complexities of sustainable growing. We deliver the infrastructure, IoT tools, and data-driven insights necessary to connect urban farmers, hobbyists, and wholesalers, reducing the barriers to a high-yield, organic lifestyle.
